Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my active order?

You can track your order live through our interactive map in the "Active Orders" section. You'll see real-time updates from the moment the chef starts cooking until the rider reaches your door.

What are the standard delivery hours?

Our official delivery hours are from 8:00 AM to 11:30 PM, seven days a week. However, many of our partner restaurants may have their own specific operating hours.

Do you offer contactless delivery?

Yes, safety is our priority. You can select "Contactless Delivery" during checkout. Our riders will leave your order at the doorstep and notify you via call or message.
